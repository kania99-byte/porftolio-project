## AI Powered SEO Content Production Research

This section documents the structured research phase focusing on how modern practitioners build scalable content systems using AI while maintaining organic visibility and search quality.

### 1. What Was Collected
The research artifacts have been systematically curated and organized inside the `/research/` directory with the following structure:
* **`sources.md`** : The master directory tracking 10 SEO industry experts, including verified platform links (Website, LinkedIn, YouTube), recent content dates, and deep annotations of their frameworks.
* **`research/linkedin-posts/`** : Organic insights and actionable text snippets scraped from practitioner feeds (e.g., Nathan Gotch's breakdown about SEO vs GEO).
* **`research/youtube-transcripts/`** : Structured transcript summaries pulled via API methods (e.g., Koray Tuğberk Gübür's programmatic semantic workflows).
* **`research/other/`** : Highly credible and trusted articles published directly by the experts, detailing advanced frameworks and optimization methodologies (e.g., Aleyda Solís's strategic guide on building a defensible AI Search Prompt Library).


### 2. Expert Selection Breakdown (Why Each Expert Was Chosen)
The 10 experts were chosen based on their specific, proven specialization in navigating the intersection of search algorithms, machine learning, and scaled content creation:

1. **Koray Tugberk Gubur**: Chosen for pioneering the "Topical Authority" framework and his recent technical research on managing the "Cost of Retrieval" within LLMs, making his semantic map strategies essential for feeding complex topical architectures into AI pipelines without losing engineering quality.
2. **Kevin Indig**: Chosen for his enterprise level growth insights and his recent 2026 collaborative report on the "State of AI Search" providing concrete numbers and experimental data regarding how AI search changes organic user acquisition.
3. **Lily Ray**: Chosen for her real time tracking of search engine algorithm changes and her actionable frameworks on how Google's spam detection systems filter low effort AI content floods, establishing the baseline for safe AI content generation.
4. **Aleyda Solis**: Chosen for her practical, highly regarded industry optimization checklists that detail how to build defensible AI Search prompt libraries so automated content remains structured enough to get cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini.
5. **Cyrus Shepard**: Chosen for his rigorous, controlled SEO experiments regarding internal linking and content updates, providing concrete data on how to refine raw AI drafts to satisfy core on page helpful content signals.
6. **Kyle Roof**: Chosen for his scientific approach to isolating algorithmic ranking signals, establishing exact mathematical parameters like entity placement and text density to validate AI text performance before scaling production.
7. **Ryan Law**: Chosen for his strategic editorial frameworks at Ahrefs that advocate for Human in the Loop (HITL) workflows, ensuring that scaled AI generation successfully incorporates "Information Gain" rather than mimicking existing web data.
8. **Britney Muller**: Chosen for her data science background that bridges the gap between machine learning applications and execution, demystifying how language models retrieve, vectorise, and rank content.
9. **Michal Suski**: Chosen for his product builder perspective at Surfer SEO, demonstrating live, end to end practical workflows on how to programmatically transition from raw keyword clusters into fully optimized, production-ready AI text assets.
10. **Nathan Gotch**: Chosen for his operational expertise in translating complex search strategies into standardized, repeatable SOPs used to train and quality control production teams handling AI content pipelines.
