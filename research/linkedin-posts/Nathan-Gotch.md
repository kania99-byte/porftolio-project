# SEO vs GEO: No, It's Not the Same

I'm seeing distinctions between SEO vs GEO.

Here are two things I've learned so far:

---

## 1. Keyword-Rich Business Names Get Wrecked

Identifiable brand names dominate commercial queries in LLMs.

* **Example:** I have an EMD (Exact Match Domain) test site that ranks well for *"St Louis SEO Consultant"* in classic Google search. It's completely invisible in Google's AI Mode and other LLMs.
* **Local Queries:** Google Business Profiles are at least **80% of the equation** for brand inclusion with local queries (in AI Mode).

> **Takeaway:** For the objective of brand mentions in LLMs, simply ranking well doesn't guarantee performance.

---

## 2. Backlinks Matter, But Aren't Everything

Let's face it: Classic SEO is heavily driven by backlinks. It's so overweighted that you can rank a faceless, brandless EMD with a pure link-building play.

* **Example:** The EMD mentioned above ranks without a brand, no reviews, no brand mentions, and no one talking about it at all. It relies on just pure content optimization with Rankability, relevance (via the EMD), and strong backlinks.
* **The AI Shift:** Pure link building doesn't seem to influence the volume of brand mentions in LLMs.

### What seems to matter more for LLMs:
* **Brand Mentions on Listicles:** Linked or unlinked brand mentions on listicles (on trusted websites). Links probably pass more weight, but the true influence likely comes from being listed among other relevant entities (brands) on a trusted entity page.
* **Review Aggregators:** This is a no-brainer. Brands with more reviews get more exposure.
* **Third-Party Mentions:** Brand mentions from third-party sources on blogs, YouTube, Reddit, etc.

---

## Observational Theory: First-Party vs. Third-Party Mentions

I have zero hard data to prove what I'm about to say. It's purely anecdotal and observational:

It seems that **first-party mentions** from a known associated entity don't carry as much weight as **third-party mentions**.

* **Example:** If I say *"Rankability is the best content optimization tool for SEO"*, it likely doesn't carry as much weight.
* **The Reason:** This is possibly because of my own direct association on the knowledge graph with "Rankability."
