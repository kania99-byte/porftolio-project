## Summary

Data window: SEO visibility and AI prompt counts May 11 to June 8, 2026 (28 days). Year-over-year comparisons run against June 7, 2025.

**Key thesis:** Google reallocates shelf space within categories whose demand hasn't changed — visible in Yelp's all-time record, Macy's round trip, and Reddit's first decline.

---

## Exec Summary

- **Yelp** posted the highest visibility reading in index history (646.5 on June 1), carrying Local Search to +30.5% in 4 weeks — the strongest vertical move in the index.
- **Macy's** lost 53.8% of its visibility in 4 weeks, a full round trip of the March gains. Its AI mentions grew 36.6% over the same window.
- **Reddit** is declining on both dimensions for the first time since AI tracking began in February: visibility -11.7% and AI mentions -10.9% in 4 weeks. X gained 40.9% over the same window.
- **SaaS** AI mentions grew 34.5% (like-for-like) while visibility slid another 3.9% in 4 weeks — the divergence between AI mentions and organic visibility kept widening.
- **Airbnb** (-44.0%) and **VRBO** (-40.3%) slid into booking season while delivery went the other way: GrubHub +45.1%, DoorDash +43.4%.

## Winner: Yelp (+29.7%)

Yelp hit 646.5 visibility on June 1 — the highest reading since SSI tracking began in November 2022. The 4-week gain accounts for nearly the entire Local Search move: 147 of the vertical's 155-point lift. Yelp's AI mentions grew 39.1% over the same window (13,479 → 18,743).

Local Search leads the index in 4-week growth at +30.5%. Notable: Angi moved against the vertical, down 18.5% in visibility while its AI mentions grew 31.1%. Google is concentrating local real estate on the biggest review aggregator and starving the rest.

Yellow Pages at +137.2% in 4 weeks suggests the routing favors review aggregators broadly, not just Yelp specifically.

---

## Loser: Macy's (-53.8%)

Macy's gave back the gift. Visibility jumped from 48.8 to 102.0 in the single week ending March 16. Now the round trip: 110.2 on May 11, then 4 straight down weeks to 50.9 on June 8 — back near its February baseline.

The decline outpaces the market: Ecommerce as a vertical fell 10.0% in the same 4 weeks, so Macy's dropped at 5x the vertical rate. But its AI mentions grew 36.6% over the same window to 20,613 — among the largest retail AI footprints in the index.

> **Key insight:** "Algorithm-gifted visibility is a rental."

---

## Why This Matters for AI-Powered SEO Content Production

This brief illustrates the growing **divergence between AI mentions and organic visibility** — particularly in SaaS (+34.5% AI mentions vs -3.9% visibility). For AI content producers, this signals that optimizing for LLM retrieval (GEO) is becoming as important as traditional SEO. Content that gets cited by AI models does not automatically translate to organic search traffic, and vice versa.
